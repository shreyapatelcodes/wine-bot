# Monthly Recap: 4 Learnings from Building with AI

**Posted:** ~3 months ago  
**Impressions:** 4,055 ⭐ TOP PERFORMER  
**Project:** Reflection on first month of weekly builds

---

This month, I shipped a full-stack app - multi-agent AI, vector search, auth, the works - solo, with the help of Claude Code.

Here are my 4 biggest takeaways:

1. The hard part changed.
When building becomes fast, the challenge isn't can I build this, it's should I. I caught myself saying "just one more feature" on repeat and that was the trap. Speed without intention just produces garbage. The new skill isn't building fast. It's deciding well, fast.

2. AI gets you 80%. Taste gets you the rest.
Even the best prompts produce drafts. The last 20% - the part that separates "it works" from "it's good" - still comes from testing every edge case, noticing when something feels off, and having the judgment to fix what AI doesn't know is broken. 

3. My job expanded, not disappeared.
I'm a PM. I have no clue if that job title will exist in the same way in 5 years; but, by the end of the month, I was also moonlighting as an engineer, designer, and QA. Not because I became technical overnight, the tools just let me operate across the full stack of building a product. I don't think AI is going to take my job, but it is going to turn us all into Swiss army knives.

4. Just start.
The discourse makes it seem like you need custom MCPs, soul docs, and a complex setup before you can be productive. You don't. I started with a blank repo and a simple prompt. The learning is in the building. The optimization comes later, if it comes at all.
