# Week 9: First Engineering PR

**Posted:** ~2 months ago  
**Impressions:** 2,093  
**Project:** First engineering spec and PR at work

---

I'm a PM. Last week I wrote my first engineering spec and started working on my first PR.

3 things I'm learning:

1. Start embarrassingly small. This one copy change is teaching me more about our codebase than months of reading specs ever did. You don't need a big feature to start. You need one thing that touches real code.

2. You can't do this alone. Claude helped me write it. My engineering partners are the reason it'll actually ship - they walked me through the architecture, gut-checked the implementation, made sure my testing strategy tested something real. AI gets you to the starting line. Your team gets you across it.

3. Stop asking AI to just write the code. Start asking it to explain the code. Why this approach? What could break? On my side projects, I let Claude drive. At work, I need to understand every line. So I go in loops - ask, read, ask again - until I actually get it!

These were my reflections on week 9 of building & shipping with AI! Stay tuned for more!
