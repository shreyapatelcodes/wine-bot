# Week 17: Design Crit - Figma vs Claude Design

**Posted:** ~1 week ago  
**Impressions:** 1,789  
**Project:** Design work + tool comparison

---

I never thought I'd be the person presenting my work in a design crit, but last week I got to do just that! It was really exciting to get real feedback - I learned more in that hour than in weeks of prompting Claude.

Some thoughts:

I like Figma + Claude's MCP way more than Claude Design. I think the difference is control. Claude Design gives you an output. Figma lets me iterate, try three directions, tweak things manually. It's a good tool for working within an existing design system.

For starting from scratch, though, I still prefer Google Stitch. It's fast and loose and gives you something to react to before you even know what you want.

That said, Claude does have its limits. It really fights constraints. It would rather invent than stay within the system, and it hallucinates a lot. I have to keep reminding it to use LinkedIn's components and libraries. Something I'm getting better at is spotting when it wanders off!

I still have a lot to learn here, but for week 17 of building and shipping with AI, I'm excited to keep going.
