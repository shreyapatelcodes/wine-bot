# Week 15: PhoneTag Frontend with Claude Design

**Posted:** ~3 weeks ago  
**Impressions:** 2,736  
**Project:** PhoneTag UI with Claude Design

---

I always put off calling people to schedule things. The dentist, the plumber, the DMV - offices are open while I'm at work, so I forget, and it gets pushed and pushed and pushed for days.

So I built an app that does it for me.

Phonetag is a voice agent: you type an instruction like "Call Dr. Miller's office at 212-555-0199 and schedule a cleaning for next Thursday afternoon" - and it makes the call. It works locally, and I'm still figuring out deployment, but the core is there!

This week's build was giving it a frontend that actually felt good. Claude released their new design tool last week, so I figured it was perfect timing!

Some things I liked: the work it produces is good. It also surfaces these fun sliders that let you adjust accent colours, border wobble, paper warmth. I gave it a prompt explaining what the app does, and it built me a complete frontend that worked pretty much in one shot.

Some things I struggled with: I couldn't really have a conversation with it. I'd prompt, and it would immediately start building, no questions or back and forth. I've been using Google Stitch a lot, and the ability to go back and forth there has spoiled me. With Claude Design, I ran out of tokens for the entire week within three revisions. What I have is close but not quite right.

There's a lot I still want to test. Claude Design has inline editing, drawing tools, and commenting that I haven't properly explored yet. I have a feeling the back-and-forth I was missing might live in those features rather than the main prompt. More to come on that.

Week 15 of building + shipping something with AI every week!
