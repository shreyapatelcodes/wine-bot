# Week 7: Reverie Launch

**Posted:** ~2 months ago  
**Impressions:** 2,503  
**Project:** Reverie AI dating coach (full launch)

---

You know that moment - you're on your friend's couch, three months post-breakup, replaying every conversation, and it hits you: oh. I did this exact thing last time. And the time before that.

That's the thing about dating patterns. They're obvious to everyone except the person living them. Your friends see it. Your therapist definitely sees it. You see it... eventually.

Liz Lee and I built Reverie to close that gap. It's an AI dating coach that asks you 5 honest questions about your relationship history and generates a personalized pattern report. 

Try it here: https://lnkd.in/gWdS9Fbk

Here's what you get:
- Your attachment style
- Your dating archetype 
- Red and green flags personalized to YOUR patterns (I did feel called out!)
- A cheat sheet of questions to bring on your next date

I'd love to hear what archetype you get - drop it in the comments. 

My biggest learning this week was that constraints make AI products dramatically better. Our first version was open-ended. You just chat with an AI about your love life. It felt like talking to a therapist chatbot. Forgettable. When we switched to 5 specific questions with structured follow-ups, the output went from generic to pretty sharp. 

This was week 7 of building and shipping with AI - stay tuned for more like this!
